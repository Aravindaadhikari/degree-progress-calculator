Part-Time & Apprenticeship Degree Progress & Target Calculator

Use this tool to estimate your current GPA and explore the marks you may need in your remaining Level 6 modules.

Important: This calculator provides an indicative estimate only. It should not be taken as a definitive prediction of your final degree classification. Level 4 marks do not contribute to the final classification.
How to use this calculator
Select your current stage, then enter exactly the number of marks shown. Only marks expected to have been released at that stage are accepted.
How this is calculated
One lowest module mark is disregarded across Levels 5 and 6. The final CGPA is calculated as one-third of the Level 5 average plus two-thirds of the Level 6 average. Where future marks are unknown, alternative target scenarios are shown depending on where the disregarded mark eventually falls.
Stage release pattern
PT/Apprenticeship release pattern: Y1 S1 = 0; Y1 S2 = 2 L5; Y2 S1 = 4 L5; Y2 S2 = 6 L5; Y3 S1 = 6 L5 + 2 L6; Y3 S2 = 6 L5 + 4 L6.
